# Initial media outlet coverage check

Observed 10 September 2026. This bounded sample informs outlet selection; it is not a coverage census, recognition survey, or permission audit. Publication dates are not game release dates. Samples were not equal-sized or drawn from identical windows, so they do not establish comparative output volume.

## Selected candidate evidence

- **IGN:** Official regional pages expose substantive upcoming-game previews, including [Metro 2039](https://za.ign.com/metro-2039/239790/metro-2039-the-first-hands-on-preview) and [Silver Pines](https://sea.ign.com/silver-pines/247116/silver-pines-preview-it-plays-and-looks-like-if-alan-wake-were-made-in-the-90s). The main www domain returned HTTP 403 during this check. Regional copies are evidence of IGN coverage, not separate outlets or proof of main-site ingestion feasibility.
- **Eurogamer:** The [previews archive](https://www.eurogamer.net/previews) and [Fable hands-on article](https://www.eurogamer.net/fable-preview-so-good-played-it-twice) demonstrate upcoming-game coverage; the [reviews archive](https://www.eurogamer.net/reviews) provides recent-release coverage. Platform metadata was uneven in the inspected pages.
- **GameSpot:** The [reviews index](https://www.gamespot.com/game-reviews/) provides game-specific reviews. The [Star Wars Zero Company review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/star-wars-zero-company-review/), published August 27, identifies Steve Watts and discloses over 30 hours of PC play. Its game-hub release field differs from the surrounding release context; do not treat that field as authoritative lifecycle evidence.
- **PC Gamer:** The [reviews index](https://www.pcgamer.com/reviews/) supplies a PC-focused editorial source. The inspected sample overlaps with GameSpot and GamesRadar+ on major titles. That overlap is not a finding of duplicate reporting.
- **Kotaku:** The [reviews archive](https://kotaku.com/reviews) and [Control Resonant hands-on article](https://kotaku.com/control-resonant-hands-on-giving-one-of-the-most-consistently-surprising-developers-the-benefit-of-the-doubt-2000708654) provide review and preview evidence. Section labels alone did not consistently classify article types; inspected review cards included platform and playtime information.
- **GamesRadar+:** The [reviews index](https://www.gamesradar.com/reviews/) provides another game-focused editorial source. The sampled major-release coverage overlapped with GameSpot and PC Gamer, which can support comparisons of independently expressed opinions rather than necessarily being redundant.

## Interpretation and boundaries

The evidence supports relevant coverage across all six candidates, not a measured ranking of brand recognition. Recognizability is a project selection criterion, not an empirical conclusion of this sample. Shared ownership, event access, and shared game titles do not by themselves establish either independent evidence or duplication; provenance must distinguish original assessments from republished material.

The existing [source access and reuse investigation](https://github.com/phenome/vaporstats/issues/39) covers IGN, Eurogamer, Kotaku, The Verge, and BBC, not a full conditions audit of GameSpot, PC Gamer, or GamesRadar+. Reachability is not permission. Prior permission or legal review remains outside the blanket development/launch prerequisites established by that decision; source-specific uncertainty remains explicit.

The canonical selection decision belongs to [Choose the initial media outlet shortlist](https://github.com/phenome/vaporstats/issues/48). These notes do not specify ingestion, synthesis algorithms, or production presentation.

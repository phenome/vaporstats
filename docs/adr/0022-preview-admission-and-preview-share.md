# Preview admission and the preview share of outlet and credit allowances

**Status:** Accepted

Amends the preview rules of [#79](https://github.com/phenome/vaporstats/issues/79) under [#71](https://github.com/phenome/vaporstats/issues/71). Extends [ADR 0019](0019-automatic-daily-media-run.md) and [ADR 0020](0020-later-assessment-pass.md).

## Context

VaporStats prioritizes giving every watched upcoming game an Overview. Even short descriptive coverage is enough to start one, so a preview pass should drop an article only when it is clearly not about the exact entity or is unusable or very poor in quality.

A watched upcoming game, Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons (AppID 3714800), received no preview coverage. Two independent causes were verified.

**Scheduling starved previews.** Assessment queries were ordered before preview queries and used each outlet's whole daily attempt cap. The monthly deferral reserved one search credit for every assessment search forecast to the end of the month, although the daily cap lets only a fraction of those be issued in that time. With 902 of the plan's 1,000 monthly credits left, the five due preview searches were refused, and the month's deferral checkpoint held every later preview of the month.

**The classifier rejected a genuine preview.** A GamesRadar+ write-up of a behind-closed-doors, hands-off demo, published 2026-08-26, failed three independent gates for the preview pass:

1. Its title names the game only as "Warlock". The only alias was the full name, which the body contains once, beside seven mentions of "Warlock".
2. Its title also names Baldur's Gate 3, another enrolled root, so the article was treated as cross-family coverage and rejected. Its body names Baldur's Gate 3 twice.
3. Neither its title nor its first 4,000 characters contain "preview" or "early access", and its title has no announcement word. GamesRadar+ marks it as a preview only in its `mrf:tags` metadata (`unindexedFreeform:preview`), the same field that marks its reviews (`articleType:Reviews`).

## Decision

**Subject attribution is one decision that depends on the pass.** One function, `articleSubject`, takes the pass and decides the article's exact entity for every pass.

- **Initial and later passes keep their subject attribution unchanged.** The title must name the entity by a full alias, and another enrolled root family named in the title or the first 240 characters of the body rejects the article. Short names never identify an entity in these passes. The only change is the co-titled check below.
- **Preview passes add short names.** A root entity whose name has a subtitle separator (a colon, or a spaced hyphen, en dash or em dash) also has the leading part as a *short name*, such as "warlock". A short name identifies the entity only when all of these hold:
  - the article body names one of the entity's full aliases at least once;
  - its occurrence is not the head of a different title: it is not immediately followed by a subtitle separator or by a sequel number (arabic digits or a roman numeral token), as "dune" is in "Dune: Spice Wars" and "marvel's spider-man" is in "Marvel's Spider-Man 2" (`HEAD_OF_ANOTHER_TITLE`);
  - it has at least four characters (`MIN_SHORT_NAME_CHARS`), equals no full alias of any loaded entity, and leads the names of no other loaded root, so two roots that share a lead such as "warhammer 40,000" or "total war" both get no short name (`shortNamesByRoot`).
- **Preview passes decide between named families.** When a preview-pass article's title or lead names more than one enrolled root family, the subject is the family that is both named first in the title and named most often in the body (`subjectFamily`). The article is rejected when those disagree, when two families are named first at the same title position or equally often in the body, or when the title joins two families' names only by a co-title joiner: `&`, `and`, `,`, `+`, `/`, `x`, `meets`, `vs` or `versus`, optionally with whitespace (`CO_TITLE_JOINERS`). The article is admitted only for its subject: the same article fetched for another family's preview query is rejected there.
- **Every pass:** the explicit multi-game forms still reject: versus, comparison, roundup, ranking and list titles, co-titled "X and Y" previews and reviews, and roundup or comparison framing in the lead or body. The co-titled check removes every loaded entity's full aliases from the title first, so a full name that itself contains "and" or "&", such as "Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons" or "Ori and the Will of the Wisps", does not make its own review co-titled.
- Parent and child attribution within a family is unchanged.

Review qualification for the initial and later passes is otherwise unchanged: an article must be a review or an explicit hands-on preview, and announcements are refused.

**A preview pass admits any coverage of its exact entity except what it refuses.** One function, `previewPassRefusal`, lists what a preview pass refuses: a review (left for the released-game flow), patch or update notes and roadmaps, guides and walkthroughs, roundup, list, ranking, comparison and other multi-game pieces, review-related news, and developer-authored pages. A title word that also describes one game's own news ("beats", "highest", "surpasses") and a conjunction followed by the entity's own subject matter ("and the cost of magical power") are not evidence of a second game (`MULTI_GAME_PREVIEW_TITLE`, `CO_TITLED_PREVIEW`). An unusable article (`MIN_USABLE_ARTICLE_CHARS`, unchanged) is refused before any of these. The words "preview" or "early access" and an announcement word are no longer required. The article is typed `preview` when its title contains the word "preview", it shows explicit hands-on evidence, or it carries its outlet's own preview marker, even when its title also has an announcement word; it is typed `announcement` only when its title has an announcement word and none of those; otherwise `preview` (`previewArticleType`). It is hands-on only on the existing explicit hands-on evidence. An outlet's own preview marker, such as GamesRadar+'s `unindexedFreeform:preview`, is evidence that the article is a preview and never evidence of hands-on.

**Due preview queries run first, within a daily share of each outlet.** In a scheduled run, due preview queries precede assessment queries; each group is still ordered oldest due time first, then by entity. Preview-pass discovery attempts on an outlet (searches, candidate fetches, redirects and failures, counted like the daily cap counts them) are limited to 10% of that outlet's effective daily attempt cap per UTC day, rounded down and at least 1 (`PREVIEW_DAILY_SHARE_PERCENT`): 20 at the production setting of 200, 3 at the default of 30. Outlet access enforces it in `reserveOutletAttempt`, so the scheduled run and the scoped preview replay obey it alike. A preview query that meets the share is held with the day-scoped reason `preview_daily_share`, like an Outlet day stop for that outlet's previews only: it reopens on the next UTC day, and it never stops or delays the outlet's assessment queries in the same run. The overall daily cap, Outlet denials, an outlet's HTTP 429 and Provider stops apply to previews unchanged. A processing fetch of an admitted preview article counts toward the daily preview share, because the attempt log records it under its source's pass, and is never held by it, so the processing of assessment articles on the same outlet is never held either. Because processing fetches are never held, they can take an outlet's preview attempts past the share on a day; the share then holds that day's remaining preview discovery.

**Previews have a monthly share of search credits.** Preview searches may use 10% of the Tavily plan limit that the usage endpoint reports for the UTC month (`PREVIEW_MONTHLY_CREDIT_SHARE_PERCENT`; 100 of 1,000). Preview searches served this month are counted from the attempt log with the daily cap's served-attempt rule. While they are below the share, the review reservation never defers previews: each needs only a remaining credit. Once the share is used, the existing rule applies unchanged: previews are deferred when the remaining credits minus the review reservation are fewer than the pending preview queries, both read from the current durable state when the check runs, so preview queries already searched earlier in the same run are not pending, the month is recorded as deferred, and previews resume at the first daily media run of the next UTC month, subject to the same check. A deferral recorded for the current month holds previews only once the share is used, so a checkpoint written under the earlier rule holds nothing while previews remain within their share; no migration or data correction is needed. When the usage endpoint reports no plan limit, the share is 0 and the reservation applies from the first preview search. The status projection of the next preview search, which has no usage reading of its own, uses the plan limit the latest scheduled discovery run recorded.

**Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons is published.** AppID 3714800 joins the roots whose media coverage is public behind `MEDIA_OVERVIEW_PUBLIC`.

### Amended acceptance criteria of #79

- **Search priority and free credits, "Decision (2026-11-17): review searches take priority over preview searches"**, including its sub-criteria **"In each daily media run, every due initial-pass and later-pass query is ordered before every preview query."** and **"No review-pass query ever waits for a preview query."**: replaced by preview-first ordering within the daily preview share. An assessment query never waits for a preview query held by the share, but a preview query served first can use credits or attempts an assessment query would otherwise have used.
- **Deferral** ("Preview searches are deferred when those credits, minus the credits needed for review-pass queries that are due or will fall due before the end of the UTC month, are fewer than the due preview queries."): applies only after the month's preview credit share is used.
- **"A multi-day backlog test shows review passes progressing ahead of preview occurrences, the deferral starting and ending at the UTC month boundary, and no review pass waiting for a preview query."**: the backlog test shows previews first within their daily share, assessment queries served the same day, and the deferral only after the preview credit share is used.
- **"Outlet denials, Outlet day stops, Provider stops, and the 30-attempt per-outlet UTC-day cap apply unchanged and are shared with every other pass, stage, and correction."**: they still apply to preview occurrences, and the daily preview share adds a preview-only hold within the per-outlet cap.
- **Admission and qualification, first criterion** ("A preview occurrence admits hands-on previews, non-hands-on previews, and announcement coverage of the exact entity, under the existing exact-subject rules."): a preview occurrence admits any coverage of the exact entity that `previewPassRefusal` does not refuse; preview, hands-on or announcement wording is no longer required, and for the preview pass "the existing exact-subject rules" now include short names and the multi-family rule above. The exact-subject rules of the initial and later passes are unchanged apart from the co-titled check.

### Amended statements of #71

- **Summary, "Previews for watched upcoming games"**: "Review searches take priority over preview searches for free search credits." Previews now come first within their daily share of each outlet and their monthly share of search credits; beyond the credit share, review searches keep priority through the deferral.
- **User story 52**: "As a maintainer, I want review searches to take priority over preview searches, and preview searches deferred when free credits run short, so that previews never delay reviews." Previews can now use attempts and credits within their shares that an assessment query would otherwise have used; beyond those shares, previews never delay reviews.
- **"Decision (2026-11-17): review searches take priority."** ("In each daily media run, every due initial-pass and later-pass query comes before every preview query.") and its **Deferral** ("If those credits, minus the credits needed for review-pass queries that are due or fall due before the end of the UTC month, are fewer than the due preview queries, preview searches are deferred to the next UTC month."): amended as in #79 above.
- **Tavily free credits (former 8)**: "review searches take priority over preview searches. When free monthly credits run short, preview searches are deferred to the next UTC month." Amended as in #79 above.

## Consequences

- An outlet's first on-subject candidate now completes that outlet for the preview occurrence, since almost any coverage of the entity qualifies. A better candidate later in the same result list is not considered for that occurrence.
- A preview query can be held by the daily share after a few refused candidates and resumes on the next UTC day, unless a newer occurrence supersedes it first.
- Preview discovery never takes more than 10% of an outlet's daily cap, and preview searches take more than 10% of the month's plan credits only when the review reservation leaves room for them. Processing fetches of admitted preview articles count toward the daily preview share and are never held by it, including their resends.
- In a preview pass, a short name can match an unrelated use of the word only when the body also names the entity in full and the word does not head another title, and a title that leads with another family is still rejected unless that family also dominates the body. Assessment passes keep their full-name attribution, so a review titled only with a short name is not found by an initial or later pass.
- A deferral checkpoint written before this decision stops holding previews immediately while they are within their share.
